Knowledge tools

Reddit saves to a Zettelkasten

600+ saved posts I never reopened, turned into an Obsidian Zettelkasten that runs locally. Each post becomes one note with its top comments and media. TF-IDF keywords become linked concept notes, cosine similarity fills each note's "related" list, and PageRank plus centrality give every node a weight. Community detection groups concepts into "syndicates", so the graph reads as themes, not a hairball. Content hashing means a re-run never overwrites my own edits.
